Off (default): the game talks through a public message broker over HTTPS. Works on 4G/5G, no TURN needed. On: direct WebRTC, lower latency, but fails behind carrier NAT.
Free credentials: metered.ca (500 MB/month). Saved on this device, applied to the next room you create or join. Force relay is for testing: it fails fast if the TURN credentials are wrong.
